Dial Intelligence turns your own call history into clear guidance on the best local time to call every state.

Dial Intelligence

Two reps. The same list. A different hour.

Two reps, the same list, the same script, the same pitch. One books meetings. The other leaves voicemails all day.

Most sales leaders look for the answer in the rep, the script, or the list. Often it is none of those. It is the clock. A number dialled at 7:40 in the morning in the prospect’s own time zone behaves nothing like the same number dialled at 2 in the afternoon.

Every outbound team knows this in theory. Almost none of them act on it, because the data needed to act on it is buried in a call log nobody reads. That is the gap Dial Intelligence was built to close.

The hidden complication

Calling in business hours is harder than it sounds

It sounds like a solved problem. It isn’t. Your dialler records the time the call happened on your clock; your prospect experienced it on theirs. Between the two sits a conversion that goes wrong in several quiet ways.

01

Time zones shift

The continental US spans four main time zones. The gap shifts twice a year as daylight saving starts and ends, on different dates in different places.

02

Area codes are imperfect

Area codes can straddle state lines, sit in states that observe daylight saving only in part, or point nowhere at all for toll-free numbers.

03

Numbers move with people

Numbers get ported. The area code tells you where a number was issued, not where the person woke up this morning.

04

Clean reports can be wrong

Get any of this slightly wrong and your report still looks clean. It is just describing the wrong hour.

Your data, made useful

It learns from your calls, not someone else’s

01

Reads your call history

Dial Intelligence connects to your calling platform and reads every dial, outcome and timestamp from your own call history.

02

Finds the prospect’s real hour

It resolves each number to a real place, converts every call to the moment the prospect experienced it, and corrects for daylight saving on that date.

03

Maps the best window

For each state and hour of the day, you see how likely a dial is to reach a human: your pipeline, your list and your reps.

04

Shows both clocks

The dashboard shows every hour in the prospect’s local time and your team’s time, so managers can plan shifts without doing the maths in their head.

Evidence, not noise

It tells you when not to trust it

Any tool can print a percentage next to a state name. A percentage based on four calls looks exactly like one based on four thousand, and that is how teams end up rebuilding a shift pattern around noise.

01

Evidence on every recommendation

Every recommendation carries the number of calls it is based on and the model’s confidence in the result.

02

Strong signals are clear

A strong signal is marked as one, so your team knows when there is enough evidence to act.

03

Thin signals stay honest

A thin signal is labelled as a place to gather more data, rather than a place to make a decision.

04

Honest uncertainty

We would rather show an honest “we don’t know yet” than a confident number you build a quarter around.

Guidance that adapts

It keeps itself current

01

Retrains overnight

Calling patterns move when you enter a new state, buy a different list or change your hours. Dial Intelligence pulls the previous day’s calls and retrains overnight.

02

Checks every new model

Before a new model goes live, it is scored against the one currently running. If it does not perform better, it does not get promoted.

03

Improves without disruption

The existing model stays in place when it is stronger, so improvement is automatic and regression cannot happen by accident.

Built for outbound teams

Who this is for

If your team dials across multiple US states, runs shifts from outside the US, or has ever argued about the best time to call without data to settle it, this is built for you. It is particularly useful for offshore sales floors, where every hour of the shift is an expensive bet on a time zone.

You do not need to change your dialler, your CRM or the way your reps work. The guidance sits alongside what you already have, and the only thing it asks for is the call history you are already generating.

The cheapest improvement available to most outbound teams is dialling the same list at a better hour. Want to see what your own call history says?

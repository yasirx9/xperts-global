Case studies

Case study · Sales CRM

Built for our own sales floor

The challenge
Our B2B and B2C teams were tracking leads across spreadsheets and inboxes. Follow-ups slipped, and managers had no live view of who was working which lead.
What we built
A central pipeline with lead ownership, priority and stale-lead flags, daily task lists and agent activity reporting.
The result
Every lead now has an owner and a next step, and managers review pipeline and closed sales from one dashboard.
Case study · HRMS CRM

One system for people operations

The challenge
Attendance, leave approvals and payroll lived in separate tools, which made month-end slow and error-prone as the team grew.
What we built
An HRMS with an employee directory, daily attendance, approval workflows, payroll status and a recruitment pipeline.
The result
HR runs attendance, approvals and payroll from one place, and team leads see headcount and attendance in real time.
